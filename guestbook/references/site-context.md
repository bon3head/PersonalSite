# Site context — the portfolio the guestbook lives in

Justin Poli's portfolio site, built with the Astryx design system. Production is live at justinpoli.is-a.dev (commit 46d6a34, shipped 2026-11-05 ~7:53 PM EST). This file is the context a builder needs; every claim here is quoted from `spec/astryx/DESIGN.md` or the code, not from memory.

## Concept

One WebGL field of 6,144 lanes (one per CUDA core of the RTX 3070 Ti), one draw call. The field morphs between scenes by changing four uniform weights. Routes:

- `/` server: lanes parallel, warps of 32, stack words drift out left.
- `/leaflink`: ink streets on paper, mutual aid, never "event discovery".
- `/sidequests`: chalk campus plan on blueprint blue, 3 quest trails.

Only nav is the bottom-edge keymap (1 server, 2 leaflink, 3 sidequests, w whoami, c contact) as real `<a href>` links. Document height equals viewport height at every route. `window.__lanefield.laneCount === 6144` everywhere.

## The field is 3D

Lanes have x/y/z positions. The camera has a position, target, and field of view (`src/world/camera.ts`, `src/world/layouts/server.ts`). The coarse server camera (DESIGN 1.4.0, shipped 2026-11-05): pos [-3.5, 5.0, 12.0], target [0.3, 0.0, -3.0], fovDeg 52, shiftNdc [0.0, -0.3], pitch -17.91. The fine (desktop) server camera is unchanged. The SideQuests camera band is pitchMin -75 / pitchMax -5 (enforced by clampSideQuestsPitch on the SideQuests path only). There is no server camera band.

## Motion the site already has

- Morphs between 3D scene layouts (server, leaflink, sidequests, scatter).
- Bulge/ripple effects on LeafLink and SideQuests.
- Stochastic/randomized game-style motion (Justin's preference: unique every time, entropy/parallax, morphs flush, a seamless shape-shifter transformation).
- Camera tracking on mobile.
- GLITTERBOX (DESIGN 6.13, `src/chrome/glitterbox.ts`): press and hold anywhere on the lane field (or hold the `g` key) and the lanes come apart into glitter around the touch, swirling with a seeded direction; let go and they re-form. No tab, no page, no copy, no new asset. It is a mode of the one material the site is made of. GLITTERBOX is NOT used in the guestbook (axed 2026-11-09).

## Brand rules (DESIGN.md sections 9/10)

No photos, no illustrations, no icons except the built-in external-link indicator, no third-party logos, all visuals procedural. Exactly one data-brand-mark each on LeafLink (logo.png from bon3head/LeafLink) and SideQuests (sidequests.gif from opensourcery/sidequests; sidequests-alpha.gif is DO NOT USE), max 12% of viewport; none on server or whoami.
