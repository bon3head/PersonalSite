<example>
Input: A contact sheet that opens over the lane field.
Output: The sheet opens as a camera move, not a slide: the camera dollies in toward the field (300ms, Lusion main bezier) and the lanes part in 3D around the sheet, each lane taking a seeded random depth offset so the parting is never the same twice. The name field is a single line; as the visitor types, the lanes spell the name back in 3D, each letter a lane bending toward the camera. Submitting locks the name in: the camera pulls back to wide (the oklou pull-back reveal) and the new mark settles into the field at a random depth, a permanent 3D object among the lanes.
</example>
<example>

No labels above inputs, no rectangular submit button, no success toast. No two signs share the same seed, so no two feel identical.
