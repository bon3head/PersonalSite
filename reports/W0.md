## 2. Diagnostic camera readout (ruling step 1a, the one allowed change)

No `window.__lanefield.camera` existed. The existing getter
`window.__lanefield.cameraPose()` returns the rig pose only, and it
misses the SideQuests walk lean: the lean calls
`lanefield.camera.lookAt` directly after the rig pose is applied
(src/world/sidequests.ts:440), so the rendered camera differs from
the rig pose during every walk. A readout built on cameraPose alone
would have reported the wrong camera for the exact frames that
matter.

Added (uncommitted, documented here):

- src/world/types.ts:44: `readonly camera` on LanefieldDebug:
  pos, forward, pitchDeg, yawDeg, distance, groundTarget,
  projection ("perspective"), fovDeg.
- src/world/debug.ts:43: `get camera()` computing the snapshot from
  the rendered camera world matrix. Forward is the negated third
  column of the world matrix (the camera local minus Z axis).
- src/world/lanefield.ts:397: `getCameraMatrix` dep, which refreshes
  the camera world matrix before returning its elements.
- src/world/stub.ts:96: the same getter derived from the token pose
  (the stub draws nothing, so there is no rendered camera there).

Definitions, fixed for W1 and W7:

- pitchDeg is the elevation of the forward vector above horizontal
  in degrees: negative looks down, flat top-down is -90.
- yawDeg is atan2(forward.x, forward.z) in degrees.
- distance is the distance along the view ray to the y = 0 plane,
  null when looking level or up. At rest on sidequests the ray
  passes through the origin target, so distance equals the
  camera-to-target distance.
- The getter is read-only and allocates nothing on the render path;
  it runs only when sampled.

Checks on the readout itself: `npx tsc --noEmit` passes,
`node tools/lint-tokens.mjs` reports clean,
`node tools/banned-scan.mjs src` reports clean. Two lint failures
were caught and fixed during W0 (a bare number in a comment, then
the banned word "live" in a comment); both are fixed in the working
tree.
